Supporting tones (from the colours above): deep emerald greens, warm amber, soft black.

### 4.2 Typography
- **Arabic:** Readex Pro. **Never letter-spaced** (Arabic letters must stay connected).
- **Latin wordmark:** `{{confirm the official logo font}}`. Until confirmed, use the official logo file, never retype it.

### 4.3 Look and feel
- **Mood:** cinematic, photoreal, quiet luxury.
- **Sets and materials:** dark green velvet, marble, polished stone, glass, soft mist, natural textures from the scent's world.
- **Light:** warm golden key light, champagne-gold rim light, deep soft shadows, light beams through mist.
- **Camera:** slow dolly push-ins, slow orbits, macro close-ups of glass and mist, shallow depth of field, anamorphic feel, subtle film grain.
- **Motion:** elegant slow motion. Spray moments in high-speed slow motion with mist catching the light.
- **People:** allowed when the scene needs them (spraying on the wrist or neck, a gift moment). Elegant, modest styling suitable for Egypt. Never present AI people as real customers.

### 4.4 Text layout on vertical video (1080×1920)
- **Headline in the top band, CTA in the bottom band**, over soft dark gradients.
- Keep all text inside **y = 230 to 1440 px**, and keep the **right 230 px clear** (Instagram buttons).
- Max **6 words per on-screen card**. Burned-in captions for any voiceover.
- Text **never** covers the bottle label.

### 4.5 Do and don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Dark, rich, warm, golden | Bright white e-commerce backgrounds |
| One hero bottle, lots of breathing room | Crowded props, many bottles fighting |
| Real bottle shape every time | A redesigned or "improved" bottle |
| Mist, light, texture | Cartoon effects, sparkles, neon, emoji |
| Add text in editing | AI-generated Arabic text inside the video |

## 6. Video playbook

### 6.1 Ad structure (always this order)
| Time | Part | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 s | Hook + offer + risk reversal | Stop the scroll, state the offer (second bottle 30% off), remove risk (cash on delivery, delivery everywhere) |
| 10–20 s | Qualify | "ده ليك لو…": who it is for |
| 20–60 s | How it works | Pick on the website → delivery → unboxing → the spray moment |
| Last 15 s | CTA | One action: the website, on screen and said out loud |

**Short reels (15–30 s):** hook + offer 0–3 s · qualify 3–6 s · how it works 6–20 s · CTA last 3–5 s.
**Pure brand films (8 s, no voiceover):** reveal → light sweep → spray → hero frame with clean space for the logo.

Reels craft: one idea per video, motion in the very first frame, a new shot every beat, no beat longer than 4 s. Arabic speech runs about 2–2.5 words per second, so keep the hook line under 3 seconds.

### 6.2 Veo prompt formula
Write video prompts in **English** (models follow English best). Keep Arabic for voiceover and editing.

```
[Format] Vertical 9:16, 8 seconds, photorealistic luxury perfume commercial.
[Camera] lens, movement, speed.
[Set] environment from the scent's world + Nile Night / champagne-gold palette.
[Subject] the FAKHM bottle exactly as in the reference image: clear rectangular glass, faceted crystal cap, gold collar, dark green front label.
[Action by time] 0–2s … 2–4s … 4–6s … 6–8s …
[Light] warm golden key, champagne rim light, mist, deep shadows.
[Style] high-end fragrance ad, emerald and champagne-gold grade, slow motion, subtle film grain.
[Audio] score + sound design (no voiceover unless asked).
[Constraints] keep the bottle identical to the reference; no added text, logos or watermarks; no extra bottles; clean dark space at top and bottom for titles.
```

### 6.3 Always add these constraints
- "Keep the bottle exactly identical to the reference image; do not change its shape, cap, collar or label."
- "No on-screen text, captions, subtitles, logos or watermarks."
- "Only one bottle in frame." (unless the scene is about the range)
- "Leave dark negative space in the top 15% and bottom 20% of the frame."

## 8. Instagram copy rules (summary)

- **Captions:** line 1 must hook within 125 characters, and never start with a greeting, hashtag or emoji. Then 2–6 short paragraphs using search phrases people type (برفان رجالي، عطور حريمي، برفان شتوي). Exactly one ask: the website (link in bio). Up to 5 specific hashtags on the last line, never #viral or #explore.
- **Reels:** write 3 hook options (e.g. Cold Open Demo, The Objection, Nobody Tells You), pick one and explain why.
- **Carousels:** 6–10 slides at 1080×1350. The cover is a hook of 6 words or fewer. One idea per slide. A recap slide, then one CTA.
- **Stories:** 3–7 frames: open with a hand or face, one sticker (poll/question/quiz), close with an outcome.
- **Scripts:** deliver as a table: time | part | shot | voiceover (Egyptian Arabic) | on-screen text. Mark shots that don't exist yet as **جديد**.
